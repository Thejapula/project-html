About the platform

Learning is better when it's shared.

PeerReviewHub brings the peer review process into your online courses. Instead of submitting work into a void, you get real feedback from real classmates — and you grow by reviewing theirs.

Simple by design

Clean screens and clear actions, made for busy students — no confusing menus or clutter.

Meaningful feedback

Rate submissions from 1–5 and write thoughtful comments that actually help classmates improve.

Visible progress

Every completed activity, submission and review adds to a clear picture of how far you've come.
